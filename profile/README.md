# Nutricheck

**A biological twin from a few drops of blood.**

We are a French company working on preventive health: at-home molecular testing, a living model of your physiology, and nutrition built around *your* biology rather than a generic plan.

Most of our code stays private. That is on purpose. We handle health data, and we would rather be careful than impressive. This page is the public face of our GitHub organization — who we are, how to reach us, and, later, anything we decide to open.

---

## What we do

A standard blood panel is useful. It is also a snapshot, read in isolation, and easy to forget.

Nutricheck starts from an at-home test (a few drops of blood, prepaid return, no lab visit) and builds a **biological twin**: hundreds of biomarkers, organ-level biological ages, and a systems view of what is out of balance. Members can also bring in lab reports they already have and connect wearables so sleep, activity, and recovery keep the model current.

From there the product is practical, not theatrical:

- recipes and meal planning matched to molecular needs, constraints, and goals
- supplement and lifestyle guidance that can be revised when biology changes
- a chat with the twin — ask about a food, a training block, or a supplement *before* you commit to it
- retesting over the year so the picture is not frozen in time

People come for longevity, performance, inflammation, weight, recovery, or a more specific focus. The focus can change.

We are not a replacement for a clinician. We are a way to see earlier, connect the dots, and act with more precision.

## How we think about it

Prevention only works if you can measure it. Cold plunges, trending diets, and the same supplement stack for everyone are not a strategy.

The platform sits where multi-omic biology — metabolomics, proteomics, lipidomics — meets clinical biomarker work, signal processing, and machine learning. The science is the point. The product is making that science usable at home.

We are based in **Montpellier, France**. Personal data is handled under European rules (GDPR). Health data is hosted with a certified health-data host. We do not sell personal information. You can ask us to delete yours.

If you want the longer “why,” read our [manifesto](https://nutricheck.eu/manifesto). The product is at [nutricheck.eu](https://nutricheck.eu).

## This organization

Repositories we make public will show up here. Apps, APIs, models, and anything tied to member data remain private.

We are not running an open-source health stack, and we will not dump internals “for the community.” If we publish something, it will be because it is meant to be used outside the company: a library, a paper companion, a spec, a tool that does not leak how we care for people’s biology.
